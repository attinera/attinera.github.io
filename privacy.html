Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

ATTINERA has no accounts. Your wardrobe, photos and plans stay in your browser on your device. We do not run a server that receives them.

What is stored, and where

  • All wardrobe data and photos are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB) on your device.
  • Settings such as your city, units, wear limits and saved stores are stored the same way.
  • Nothing is synced between devices. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Photo analysis

Clothing photos are analyzed on your device by AI models that run in your browser: one tags the item, and one removes the background and tidies the lighting. Background removal happens entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, to ATTINERA or anyone else.

ATTINERA keeps both the cleaned photo and your original photo on your device, so you can switch back with "Show original".

The AI model files are downloaded once from a public content delivery network (jsDelivr and Hugging Face) and then cached by your browser. Those services see a normal download request (such as your IP address), not your photos.

Third-party services

  • Weather: forecast requests send an approximate location (the coordinates of your chosen city, or of your device rounded to about 1 km) to Open-Meteo. City search sends the text you type to Open-Meteo's geocoding service.
  • Fonts: the Inter typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which sees a normal font request (such as your IP address).
  • Store logos: store icons are requested from Google's favicon service, which sees a normal image request.
  • Store and product links open the retailer's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Affiliate links (future)

In the future, some shop links may be affiliate links. If so, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you, and this will be clearly disclosed.

Export or erase your data

Open the Me tab. Export downloads one file with everything, including photos. Erase all data deletes everything from this browser. You can also clear site data in your browser settings.
